Bhakti Kutir · Palolem Beach · South Goa

Getting There

Finding Your Way to Moon

Bhakti Kutir sits at the southern tip of Goa, a short walk from Palolem Beach. There are four ways in — by air, by train, by bus, or by driving down yourself. Cabs are available from all arrival points. Please note that there is no parking available at the venue, so if you are driving, plan to drop off and park nearby.

Take the Train
