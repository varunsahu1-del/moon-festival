Moon Eats · 27–30 Nov 2026

Moon is a festival where people become family — and we believe food is at the heart of that experience. We are drawn to food with real ingredients, honest cooking, and flavour that nourishes. Café owners, home kitchens, independent cooks — we welcome anyone who cooks thoughtfully.

Who's Eating

Meals are an optional add-on — and most attendees choose to eat at the venue. Brunch and dinner are served on-site across all 3 days, and the majority of the crowd opts in. The rest — approx. 100 a day — are day-pass holders, flea vendors, and attendees grazing through the day.
