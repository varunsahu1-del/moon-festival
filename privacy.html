Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Moon Yoga Adventure collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website, purchase tickets, or attend Moon Festival 2026. We take your privacy seriously and will never use your data in ways you would not expect.

01

Who We Are

Moon Festival 2026 is operated by Moon Yoga Adventure, a festival held at Bhakti Kutir, Palolem, South Goa, India. For any questions relating to this policy, please contact us at moonyogaadventures@gmail.com.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly — including your name, email address, phone number, and payment details when you register or purchase a ticket. We also collect details submitted through participation and application forms. When you visit our website, we may gather basic usage data such as pages visited and device type through cookies.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process ticket purchases and bookings, send you event updates, schedules, and logistical information, respond to enquiries and application submissions, improve our website and communications, and comply with legal obligations. We will not use your data for any purpose unrelated to Moon Festival without your consent.

04

Email Communications

If you sign up to our mailing list or purchase a ticket, you may receive updates about Moon Festival 2026. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send. We will never send you unsolicited marketing from third parties.

05

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We may share it with trusted third-party service providers — such as ticketing platforms, payment processors, and email delivery services — solely to operate the festival. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and may not use your data for any other purpose.

06

Photography & Media

By attending Moon Festival, you acknowledge that photographs and video footage may be taken during the event for editorial and promotional use by Moon Yoga Adventure. If you appear in any content you wish to be removed from, please contact us at moonyogaadventures@gmail.com and we will do our best to accommodate your request.

07

Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Once your data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

08

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise any of these rights, please write to us at moonyogaadventures@gmail.com. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable timeframe.

09

Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies required for the site to function, and optional analytics cookies that help us understand how visitors use the site. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the site.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to check back periodically.

11

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact Moon Yoga Adventure at moonyogaadventures@gmail.com or write to us at Bhakti Kutir, Palolem, South Goa, India.
