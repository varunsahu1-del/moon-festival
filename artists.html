The Practitioners

Devoted people.
Living practices.

Moon Festival gathers practitioners who are true — not because they are famous, but because they are devoted.

Sound Healer

Nadia Okonkwo

A Nigerian-British practitioner working with crystal bowls, voice, and West African communal toning. Her sessions dissolve the boundary between performer and participant.

🇳🇬 Lagos / London
Ecstatic Dance DJ

Kiran Das

A Bangalore-based DJ and music therapist who weaves Indian classical rhythm with ambient electronic textures — his sets build over three hours into something approaching trance.

🇮🇳 Bangalore
Breathwork Guide

Sofia Andersson

Trained in Holotropic Breathwork under Stanislav Grof's lineage, Sofia facilitates journeys many describe as the most profound experiences of their lives — without any substance.

🇸🇪 Stockholm
Yoga & Somatic Movement

Meera Pillai

A Kerala-trained yoga teacher and somatic practitioner who integrates traditional Hatha lineage with trauma-informed movement. Her morning classes begin at sunrise.

🇮🇳 Kerala
Plant Medicine Ceremonialist

Tomas Vega

A Colombian-born ceremonialist trained in the Shipibo tradition, Tomas holds space for plant medicine work with devotion and precision honed over twelve years of apprenticeship.

🇨🇴 Medellín
Live Sacred Music

Ananda Collective

A seven-piece ensemble blending Kirtan, jazz improvisation, and West African percussion. Their Earth Circle sets have become Moon Festival legend.

🌍 International
Speaker & Philosopher

Dr. Rina Mathur

Author of The Ecology of Self, Dr. Mathur brings neuroscience and lived spiritual practice into conversation that is rigorous, warm, and deeply human.

🇮🇳 Delhi
Ecstatic Dance Facilitator

Jasmine Ho

Jasmine holds ecstatic dance spaces that are also consent workshops — she teaches participants how to move, how to be moved, and how to hold the difference between the two.

🇸🇬 Singapore
Gong & Tibetan Bowl Artist

Orion Blackwood

With 40 hand-hammered Tibetan bowls and 12 planetary gongs, Orion's sound baths are designed as complete journeys — you enter standing and often leave horizontal.

🇬🇧 Bristol
Cacao Ceremonialist

Valeria Cruz

Trained by Mayan lineage holders in Guatemala, Valeria brings ceremonial cacao with a reverence that honours its origins and its power as a heart-opening medicine.

🇬🇹 Guatemala City
Pranayama & Wim Hof

Param Singh

A Wim Hof method instructor and Kundalini Pranayama teacher, Param leads cold-water immersions at dawn — one of Moon Festival's most talked-about experiences.

🇮🇳 Rishikesh
Electronic & Ambient

Luna Osei

A Ghanaian composer whose music exists between lullaby and ceremony — she builds immersive sonic landscapes for the late-night Forest Temple stage.

🇬🇭 Accra
