Gather · Sit · Eat

Food as a ritual

At every mealtime, everyone comes together — strangers become family, seated on the ground in a circle. The meals are designed with intention — light, creative, easy to digest — so your body stays light and your energy stays present for the workshops. Food here is its own practice — a daily ritual of slowing down.

Tribal Lunch Experience

Optional Add-on

Available All Three Days · 27, 28 & 29 November

Tribal women from deep inside the forest — carrying generations of knowing in their hands. No oil. No shortcuts. Just wood fire, earthen pots, and time. The recipes are old — passed through South Indian tribal lineages, the spices are gathered from the forest floor, food is served on jackfruit leaves, slow-cooked. The simplicity is the point.

We have capacity for 70 people per day only. Tribal Lunch can be added as an add-on during checkout.
