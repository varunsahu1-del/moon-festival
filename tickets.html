Common Questions

Your ticket includes a 3-night / 4-day stay at your chosen venue, plus full access to all workshops, festival acts, music events, ceremonies, and talks across all three days.

Check-in is at 2 PM on 27th November. Check-out is 30th November at 11 AM.

If you'd like to arrive a day earlier, an Early Check-in add-on for 26th November is available — you can add this when booking your ticket.

Meals are not included in the base ticket but can be added on. We offer Satvik brunch & dinner for all festival days — ₹1,000 per day, ₹3,000 total for 27th, 28th & 29th November.
Full access to all workshops, music acts, ceremonies, and talks across all three festival days — 27, 28 & 29 November. Accommodation is not included; you'll need to arrange your own stay nearby.
Add-ons are available with the Stay + Festival Pass only — they are not available for the Festival Only ticket and cannot be booked during the festival. All add-ons must be prebooked at checkout.

Early Check-in · 26 Nov — arrive a day early; inauguration at 7pm that evening. Highly recommended. If you skip this, note that on 27 Nov hotel check-in is only from 2pm — the first workshop begins at 7am that morning. That's a long wait with nowhere to go. Limited spots.

Meals · 27–29 Nov — Satvik brunch & dinner daily, eaten together as a ritual. ₹3,600 total (6 meals). First 70 only.

Tribal Lunch — cooked by tribal women from the forests of South Goa. Included with meals; choose any one day (27, 28 or 29 Nov). First 70 only.

Ayurvedic Massage — traditional full-body massage. Save ₹300 vs on-site price. First 50 only.
All prices displayed are exclusive of GST. 5% GST will be added at checkout.
