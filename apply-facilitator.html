Moon · 27–30 Nov 2026

Moon is an annual gathering which returns to the beaches of South Goa from 27–30 November 2026. Workshops are a central part of the experience — and for facilitators, it's one of the best places to connect directly with people who are genuinely curious about your work. Many leave with new students, clients, and collaborators.

We invite facilitators to contribute sessions across Yoga, Art, Fitness, Healing, Music, Dance, Movement & Wisdom — disciplines that encourage learning, wellbeing, expression & exploration.

How It Works

3 Days
20 Facilitators
3 Workshops Each

Every year, 20 facilitators bring Moon to life — each leading 3 workshops across the 3 days, one a day.

What Moon Offers

All Access Pass
Accommodation
Meals

Moon offers each facilitator triple sharing AC accommodation for 4n / 5d, brunch and dinner for 3 days of the festival & an All Access pass to Moon 2026 — all workshops, acts, talks & ceremonies.
