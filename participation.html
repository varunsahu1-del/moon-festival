Moon Festival thrives because of its community of makers, movers, healers, and dreamers. Whether you want to lead a workshop, sell your craft, create an installation, or be part of the crew — we would love to have you.

Lead sessions in yoga, dance, music, or healing arts. We welcome teachers who bring depth and an embodied understanding of their craft.
