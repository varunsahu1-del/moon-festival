Moon Art · 27–30 Nov 2026

At Moon, art doesn't hang on walls — it grows from the ground. Every stage, structure, entrance, and detail is built from bamboo, leaves, rope, earth, and found material — we don't bring in what the land doesn't offer. We invite installation artists, light artists, sculptors, carpenters, and spatial practitioners who feel the same way — rooted in the natural world.
