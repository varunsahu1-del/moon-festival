What is Moon

A festival of workshops, music, and belonging.

Moon is three days in South Goa built around 75+ workshops led by 25+ facilitators, with 20+ live musicians — fitness, healing, yoga, arts, music, dance & ceremonies — held together by a rare sense of community.

Ways to partner

4 ways of partnering with Moon

Title Partner · 1 availableMoon, co-presented by you
Your name in the festival lockup and on every piece of creative, the main stage, and the largest on-ground presence. The face of Moon 2026.
Wellness Partner · 1 availablePresents the workshops
Own the heart of Moon — all 9 shalas and 75+ workshops, the teacher line-up, and the schedule. Ideal for a wellness, nutrition, or lifestyle brand.
Entertainment Partner · 1 availableThe Moon Stage, by you

Sponsor a specific experience that fits your product — e.g. a nutrition brand on the fitness & yoga block, a beverage on the morning circles, a label on the Moon Market.
What a partnership includes

The community. The marketing.

Moon is a community first. A partnership puts your brand inside it — for three days, with the people who built it.

  • Residential passes — your founder or leadership team lives the festival, not visits it
  • The partners' table — a private lunch during the festival with the Moon founders, the owners of the venues, our headline teachers and the other partner brands
  • Curated sessions built for visiting leadership teams — from wellbeing to financial hygiene
  • Complimentary passes for your wider team
  • A brand-built experience — the morning ritual, a workshop, a conversation
  • Three days of real product use — in rooms, in hands, in sessions
  • A dedicated installation — a photo-op built into the festival world
  • A stall at the Moon Market — sampling & welcome-kit placement
  • Brand in the festival identity — name lockup & "presented by" credit (Title), category ownership (all tiers)
  • Logo & link on the Moon Festival website
  • 500–600 posters across cafés, hotels & ATMs around Goa
  • 5,000 flyers through 25 Goa cafés
  • Ticket confirmations, mailers & the attendee WhatsApp group, co-branded
  • Dedicated Instagram — posts, stories & reels
  • Physical branding — stage backdrop, shala banners, entrance, signage & flags
  • Schedule standees at 15–20 locations
  • Logo on crew tees & the festival wristband everyone wears
  • Airport-taxi branding on guest pickups
  • Branded reels, photos & content of guests with your product
  • Recap report — footfall, reach & engagement metrics · tagged UGC
