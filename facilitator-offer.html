What Moon Offers You

Since 2024

Moon brings together 500+ people who are genuinely curious — about movement, healing, art, and each other. As a facilitator, you're not just leading sessions. You're being introduced to a community that keeps coming back, year after year. Many of our facilitators leave with new students, collaborators, and friends. It's one of the most direct ways to share your work with people who are already open to it.

What Moon Offers

In exchange for the three workshops you lead, Moon gives you a Festival Pass (₹21,000 value) — full access to all workshops, ceremonies, and experiences across the festival.

Beyond that, you choose what works best for you:

Option A — Facilitator Fee

₹10,000 cash fee for your time and offering.

Option B — Accommodation & Meals

Triple sharing stay within 5 minutes of the venue (air-conditioned), for 4 nights and 5 days, plus meals — together worth ₹15,000.
