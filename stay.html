Bhakti Kutir

Festival Venue · On Site · Non-AC

Bhakti Kutir has 26 unique cabanas — no two are alike. Fashioned from local materials like rice straw and bamboo, the cabanas are built to include trees, leaves, and to let you see the moon and star-filled skies. Our shared dorms are designed to fit naturally into the tropical forest we've nurtured over the years. Each space is open and airy, with mosquito nets, personal storage, attached washrooms, and thoughtfully arranged beds that allow a little privacy while being part of community.
